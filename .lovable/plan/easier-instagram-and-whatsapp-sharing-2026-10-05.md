# Easier Instagram and WhatsApp sharing

## Changes
- Share the generated ShopLook image through the phone’s native share menu.
- Give Instagram and WhatsApp buttons app-specific prompts so shoppers know which destination to choose.
- On browsers without image sharing, download the look and provide a clear app-specific next step.
- Keep the shopper’s generated image private and avoid uploading it merely for sharing.

## Validation
- Check both buttons with image sharing available and unavailable.
- Confirm cancelled sharing produces no error and the page still builds cleanly.
